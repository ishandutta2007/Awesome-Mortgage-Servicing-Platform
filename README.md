# Awesome-Mortgage-Servicing-Platform

## Top Mortgage Servicing Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Loan Servicing, Payment Processing, Escrow, Default Management, Investor Reporting & Borrower Self-Service*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Mortgage Servicing**. These systems manage the post-origination life of mortgage loans—collecting payments, managing escrow, handling customer service, reporting to investors/agencies, and supporting default and loss-mitigation workflows.



**Examples** include ICE MSP, Sagent, Dovenmuehle Servicing, Finastra Mortgagebot, Shaw Systems, Mortgage Automator, Calyx, LoanCare, Dark Matter, Cloudvirga, Black Knight MSP, ServiceMac, FICS Mortgage Servicer, Covius, MeridianLink Servicing, and LoanPro Mortgage (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Production-scale residential mortgage servicing platforms are almost exclusively commercial due to heavy regulation (CFPB, GSE, investor, state rules). Practical open options are limited to **general lending/loan-management systems** (Frappe Lending, OpenCBS) and experimental platforms—not full GSE-compliant MSPs. This section is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[ICE MSP](https://www.ice.com/)**  

  Major mortgage servicing platform (Mortgage Servicing Package) used by large servicers for loan accounting, payment processing, and investor reporting; expanding AI agent capabilities.



- **[Sagent](https://www.sagentlending.com/)**

- **[Dovenmuehle Servicing](https://www.dovenmuehle.com/)**

- **[LoanPro Mortgage](https://loanpro.io/)**  

  Flexible loan management and servicing platform adaptable to mortgage and consumer lending use cases.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Frappe Lending](https://github.com/frappe/lending)**  

  Open-source loan management system built on ERPNext/Frappe covering loan products, disbursement, repayment, collateral, and accounting—usable as a foundation for non-agency lending.



- **[OpenCBS](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source loan tracking software originally aimed at microfinance institutions (client, loan, savings, collateral, and schedule management).



- **[SMB / general lending platform prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental open-source platforms covering application, underwriting, servicing, and collections for small-business or specialty lending.



- **[Loan schedule and amortization open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Libraries for generating amortization schedules, interest calculations, and payment waterfalls.



- **[Payment and escrow open calculation tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open components for payment application, escrow analysis, and basic servicing math (not full regulatory engines).



- **[Borrower portal open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Simple self-service portals for viewing balances, making payments, and uploading documents.



- **[Collections workflow open frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open tools for delinquency tracking and collections case management.



- **[Document and correspondence open generators](https://github.com/)**  

  Templates and engines for generating servicing letters and notices (require compliance review).



- **[Investor reporting open templates](https://github.com/)**  

  Community templates for basic portfolio and cash-flow reporting (not GSE-standard files).



- **[Regulatory data open parsers](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools for working with public mortgage and servicing-related datasets for analysis and research.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **Frappe Lending** or similar open loan-management systems for private, specialty, or non-agency portfolios where full GSE servicing is not required.

- Building custom calculation and portal layers on open libraries while keeping core servicing on a commercial MSP.

- Accepting that agency-compliant servicing (Fannie/Freddie/Ginnie), investor reporting, default management at scale, CFPB-ready workflows, and certified payment processing still require commercial platforms (ICE MSP, Black Knight, Sagent, LoanCare, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on transparency of calculations, borrower experience prototypes, and non-regulated lending use cases.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Core loan boarding, payment application, escrow, and investor reporting must remain on validated commercial MSPs. Open tools may support ancillary portals, analytics, or specialty-loan servicing under strict compliance oversight. Most mortgage servicers continue to rely exclusively on commercial servicing platforms for regulatory and operational reasons.
